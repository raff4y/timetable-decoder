How It Works

Three Steps, One Clean Schedule

  1. 01

    Create Your Account

    Sign up with your email. An admin approves new accounts before they can open timetables.

  2. 02

    Pick Your Sections

    Choose your department’s timetable, then search by code or paste a list like EE3012-5A. Clashes are flagged as you go.

  3. 03

    Download Your Week

    Get a high-resolution PNG with rooms, times and instructors, ready to save, share or print.

Features

Built Around How FAST Timetables Actually Work

Flat course lists, room-by-period grids, labs paired with their courses and odd start times: each department’s export is read exactly as it is published.

  • Always The Published Timetable

    Admins upload each department’s official export once. No hunting for the latest spreadsheet.

  • Saved To Your Account

    Your picked sections come back on any device you log in from.

  • Clashes Caught Early

    Overlapping classes are flagged the moment you add them, and drawn side by side on the schedule.

  • Teacher Reviews, One Click Away

    Every instructor links straight to their student reviews on NUCESRate.
